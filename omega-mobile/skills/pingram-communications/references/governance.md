# Messaging governance

- Never send deceptive, coercive or impersonating messages.
- Respect recipient opt-outs and applicable SMS/email compliance requirements.
- Preserve user-provided sender identity; do not fabricate verified domains or business identities.
- Do not expose customer addresses, phone numbers, message bodies or tracking data beyond what the task requires.
- Never claim a message was delivered when Pingram only confirms request acceptance.
- Treat phone-number purchases and A2P registration as consequential account operations.
