# OMEGA v9 Meta-Architecture

The v9 layer maps organizational/architectural traits to executable mechanisms. Negotiation becomes TCO/BATNA and hard constraints; data-driven judgment becomes shadow observations and decision matrices; resilience becomes bounded retry, latency budgets and load shedding; learning becomes failure memory, drift, preference datasets and knowledge graph; strategy becomes DAG execution; uncertainty becomes confidence/HITL and clarification gates.

All mechanisms expose provenance and limitations. No component claims to alter foundation-model weights, perform federated learning, inspect hidden chain-of-thought, or provide production HNSW unless an actual external implementation is connected.
