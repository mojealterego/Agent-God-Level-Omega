# Affective and Psychological Communication

`affective-policy` accepts explicit frustration, urgency or distress signals and chooses a bounded communication style. `emotional-homeostasis` is only a numerical interaction-state controller; it reports `subjectiveFeelingClaimed=false`. For crowd psychology, interpersonal conflict, transfer/countertransference or therapeutic concepts, store hypotheses and source-backed frameworks in the dynamic knowledge layer and distinguish observation from interpretation. High-risk mental-health situations require human professional review. The system may model conversational boundaries, assertiveness and de-escalation, but it must not claim authentic empathy, attachment, desire, phenomenological consciousness or a therapist’s lived countertransference.
