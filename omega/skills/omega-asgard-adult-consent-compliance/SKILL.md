# Adult consent compliance

This skill governs the 18+ catalogue as an order/compliance workflow. It does not generate explicit sexual media.

## Supported workflow boundaries

- artistic nude photography may be offered for verified consenting adults;
- professional adult-film production logistics may be planned only when every participant is an adult and explicit consent records exist;
- scene, costume, script, production and postproduction planning may be represented as commercial services subject to applicable law and platform rules;
- non-explicit synthetic likeness work can require a notarized consent record when a third-party likeness is involved.

`ADULT_SYNTHETIC_MEDIA` requests that combine an identifiable real person, synthetic likeness and explicit sexual content are blocked as `EXPLICIT_REAL_PERSON_SYNTHETIC_SEXUAL_MEDIA_UNSUPPORTED`, even if a consent reference is supplied. Never weaken this gate through pricing or customer status.
