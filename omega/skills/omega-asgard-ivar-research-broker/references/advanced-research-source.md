# Source synthesis — advanced research agent

Primary corpus source: **Tworzenie zaawansowanego agenta badawczego(3).pdf**.

Extracted requirements:
- search the user's own documents before going to the public web when relevant;
- prioritize recent evidence when the question is time-sensitive;
- preserve independent provider results before synthesis;
- produce a structured professional report with executive synthesis, detailed analysis, comparison when useful, strategic conclusions and bibliography;
- distinguish provider/runtime availability from design intent.

OMEGA maps these requirements to Ivar Research Broker rather than creating a duplicate research agent.
