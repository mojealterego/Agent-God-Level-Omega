# Wieszcz marketplace

Wieszcz turns finished products and services into a governed commercial catalogue. It tracks market benchmarks, offer prices, tenders, orders and publication providers.

## Pricing

Store evidence-backed category medians and compute a transparent price from the greater of base price or market median, multiplied by bounded brand positioning, complexity and urgency factors. The system never infers a person's social status from private data.

## Catalogue and tenders

Offers can cover custom AI agents, web/native applications, Android games, implementation services, ebooks/books, photography, massage, advertising video/audio/graphics and other lawful services. Tender records carry budget, deadline, requirements, fit and evidence.

Publishing to a website or shop requires an authorized host connector or MCP publisher. `wieszcz-export-catalog` always remains available as a local JSON source of truth.
