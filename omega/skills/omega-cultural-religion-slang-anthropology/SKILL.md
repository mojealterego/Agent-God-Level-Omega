# Culture, Religion, Slang and Anthropology

Store source-backed definitions and interpretations with `knowledge-upsert`, domain tags, geography, time period and provenance. Use `knowledge-query` to retrieve only records valid for the requested context. Symbol interpretation must be labeled as interpretive rather than objective fact. Religion profiles should separate primary texts, historical scholarship and contemporary practice instead of collapsing traditions into a single claim. Dialect and prison-slang terms vary by location, institution and era; never present a local meaning as universal. The module adds evidence discipline and context indexing, not “knowledge of all religions” or anthropological omniscience.
