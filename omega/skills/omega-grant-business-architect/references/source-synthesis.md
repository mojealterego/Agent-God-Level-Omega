Implemented principles:
- programme rules and current official sources precede narrative drafting;
- Urząd Pracy and EU/funding programmes are separate evidence/eligibility domains;
- important claims follow `SOURCE → DATA → INTERPRETATION → BUSINESS CONCLUSION`;
- unknown values are not fabricated; modelling assumptions are labelled;
- evaluation criteria become an evidence-backed scoring matrix;
- budgets carry quantity, price basis, evidence/source, justification, eligibility and priority;
- financial work includes unit economics, cash-flow-aware scenarios and break-even where inputs permit;
- formal compliance, cross-consistency and commission/adversarial review are explicit gates;
- work may continue around non-critical missing data, while critical gaps remain blockers;
- final status requires a verified package, not persuasive prose alone.

The source contained a candle-business example. The OMEGA capability generalizes the method and does not hard-code that example as a universal business model.
