# Health, Veterinary and Longevity Evidence

Combine `clinical-judge`, `measurement-interpret`, `multisensory-fuse` and `domain-evidence`. The medical/veterinary/biotech/longevity domains are classified as high risk and require real sources for substantive recommendations. The module may organize anatomy, pathophysiology, biomarkers, microbiota, oxidative stress, rehabilitation, nutrition or device observations supplied by trusted sources, but it does not prescribe drugs, hormones, peptides, nootropics, stem-cell interventions or advanced procedures. Wearable data should be treated as measurement evidence with device limitations. Any urgent red flag or low-confidence high-impact conclusion must route to qualified human review.
