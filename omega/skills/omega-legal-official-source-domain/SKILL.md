# Legal Domain Evidence Profile

For criminal, civil, family, labour, commercial, administrative, financial/tax, constitutional, local, human-rights and treaty topics, first call `legal-context` with jurisdiction, effective date and sources. High-impact conclusions require at least one source explicitly marked official. Store current acts, regulations, Constitution material, treaty texts, Journal of Laws references or court/tribunal materials in the dynamic knowledge store with validity dates. Never represent the module as containing every law permanently in weights: law changes and source retrieval must be refreshed. Ambiguous or consequential cases require qualified legal review. The runtime does not infer missing statutory text or silently substitute another jurisdiction.
