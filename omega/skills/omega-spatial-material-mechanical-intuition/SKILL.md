# Spatial, Material and Mechanical Reasoning

Use `installation-check` for axis-aligned spatial fit, `tolerance-stack` and `measurement-interpret` for dimensional reasoning, and `engineering-rank` for candidate selection under strength, mass and cost constraints. These mechanisms can support mounting, assembly, construction layouts, mechanical fitting and material tradeoffs when real dimensions/properties are supplied. They do not replace structural analysis, fluid dynamics, finite-element analysis, laboratory material testing or a licensed engineer. “Kinesthetic”, “field”, “turbulent” or “spatial-in-motion” intuition should be implemented only when an upstream sensor/simulation provides the required numeric state.
