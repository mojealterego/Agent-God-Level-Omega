# Pingram regions

Use the same region as the Pingram account:

- US/default: `https://mcp.pingram.io`
- Canada: `https://mcp.ca.pingram.io`
- EU: `https://mcp.eu.pingram.io`

Do not silently switch regions. Account data and availability are region-scoped.

For ChatGPT, Pingram documents adding the relevant URL as a Custom MCP connector and signing in with the Pingram account.
